Random events

Bundles and skill checks appear at random while the game is open. Neither happens during offline progress.

Strange bundles

A strange bundle sometimes appears while gathering. Click it on the gathering page or the action bar before it disappears. It always gives everything below; the amounts are random.

What it gives

  • 1–2 of the spot's main resource (its first drop)
  • 🪙 40–96 coins
  • 0.5× the XP of one action

At Mine Gem Rocks it's always Stone.

Settings can also send a desktop notification when one turns up.

Skill checks

An optional bonus for watching the gathering or production page.

  • Appear while the skill's page is open and the game isn't minimized. Can be switched off in Settings.
  • A hit finishes the current action with its resource and XP. A craft finished by a hit can't miss, fail or burn.
  • Production checks come 3 to 5.5 crafts apart.
  • Each hit in a row makes the skill's actions faster: +0.4% per hit, up to +2% (⛏️ Mining, 🎣 Fishing, 🪓 Lumbering, 🌿 Herbalism, 🪤 Hunter, 🔨 Forging, 💎 Artifice, 🍖 Cooking, ⚗️ Alchemy, 🪶 Fletching, 💫 Runecrafting). A streak counts up to 5 hits.
  • A miss, or letting one run out, ends the streak.
  • One appears every 1 to 10 minutes, or 1 to 3 minutes after a hit that continues a streak (🎣 Fishing 2.7 to 4.8, 🌿 Herbalism 2.7 to 4.8).
  • Skill checks and bundles add at most 20% XP to any skill.

Sweet spot

. Hit the ✕ when it appears.

Parry

. Click or press Space as the blade crosses the shield.

Cracks

⛏️ Mining. Strike the cracks in order: 1, 2, 3.

Striking a crack out of order is a miss. Striking all three gives 1 more ore.

Hook

🎣 Fishing. Click the bar or press Space while the needle is in the green.

Better fish narrow the green zone and speed up the needle. Clicking the middle third of the green catches 1 extra fish.

Timber

🪓 Lumbering. Click when each ring turns green on the notch.

Clicking when no ring is landing is a miss. The rings come faster with better trees.

Herb patch

🌿 Herbalism. Pick the three herbs. Leave the weeds.

Touching a weed is a miss. Picking every herb gives 1 more herb.

Tracks

🪤 Hunter. Click each track before it fades.

A track that fades, or a click anywhere else, is a miss. Tracks fade faster with better animals.

Strike

🔨 Forging. Strike when each ring turns green on the metal.

Heat

🍖 Cooking. Take it off the heat while the needle is in the green, or press Space.

⚗️ Alchemy. Pop each bubble before it fades.

💎 Artifice. Pick the three gems. Leave the plain stones.
