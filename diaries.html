Achievement Diaries

Each zone has a diary: finish its tasks for coins, XP, a title and free travel there.

Ashvale Diary

🌾 Ashvale

  • Discover Ashvale
  • Reach Mining level 10
  • Defeat 10 Grave Rats
  • Reach Attack level 10

Rewards

  • 🪙 500 coins
  • 200 ❤️ Constitution XP
  • 🏷️ The title “Gravewarden”
  • 🧭 Free travel to this zone
