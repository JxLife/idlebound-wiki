Achievement Diaries

Each zone has a diary: finish its tasks for coins, XP, a title and free travel there.

🌾 Ashvale

  • Discover Ashvale
  • Defeat 100 Grave Rats
  • Defeat 150 Wild Boars
  • Defeat 150 Ragged Ghouls
  • Reach Attack level 15
  • Reach Strength level 15
  • Reach Defence level 15
  • Reach Marksmanship level 15
  • Reach Sorcery level 15
  • Reach combat level 20
  • Reach Slayer level 5
  • Reach Mining level 20
  • Reach Lumbering level 15
  • Reach Fishing level 15
  • Reach Herbalism level 15

Rewards

  • 🪙 5,000 coins
  • 2,000 ❤️ Constitution XP
  • 🏷️ The title “Gravewarden”
  • 📋 1 more queue slot
  • 🧭 Free travel to this zone
