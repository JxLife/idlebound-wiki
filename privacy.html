At a glance

IdleBound is a desktop idle RPG played through Steam. Your Steam account is your login, and your character is saved on our server. We use what we keep to run the game, show the hiscores and other players' cards, keep the game fair and help players who need support. We don't show ads, we don't use analytics or tracking, and we never sell personal information.

Who we are

IdleBound is made and run by its independent developer (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the IdleBound game, its online server, this wiki and the IdleBound Discord bot. Steam, Discord and the other services named below have their own privacy policies for what they handle themselves.

What we collect

Your account

  • Your Steam ID. When you sign in, Steam confirms who you are to our server. Your Steam ID stays on our server: other players and the game only ever see a separate player number.
  • Whether your Steam account owns the game (or plays it through Family Sharing), used only for referral rewards.
  • Your character's name, which you choose and which other players see.
  • Sign-in tokens that keep you signed in for up to 7 days.
  • When you were last online, the game version you use and which zone you are in (for the online counts on the world map).

Your character and progress

  • Your save: skills and XP, items, bank, coins, equipment, quests, achievements, pets, collection log, appearance, settings that travel with your character, your friends and blocked players, and a log of your recent actions (the last 300, such as items moved, bought or sold, deaths, travel and actions finished) that staff use to answer support questions and look into cheating.
  • Hiscores: your levels, XP, achievement points and weekly XP gains.
  • Records of trades, clan donations and dungeon runs, and the rewards our server hands out.
  • Results of our speed check, which compares each save with the last to stop cheating.
  • Clock diagnostics: saves may include up to 32 recent timing observations, event counts, game ticks, server-time comparisons, request durations, catch-up amounts and whether the game window was focused or hidden. Staff use these to investigate timing bugs and false positives. These observations do not themselves change progress or decide whether a save is accepted.

Chat, reports and staff records

  • Public chat, clan chat and party chat messages, so players who join later can see recent ones.
  • Whispers pass straight to the player they're for and are never stored.
  • Reports you make about other players, with your reason and the reported player's last few chat messages, and reports others make about you.
  • Bug reports you send, with the game's version, your zone, what you were doing, the page you were on and recent game log lines.
  • Error reports the game sends on its own when something breaks (the error, where it happened and the same game details).
  • Moderation records: mutes, bans and other staff actions on your account, and notes staff write about it.

Your PC and connection

  • Your IP address reaches our server with every request, as it does for any online service.
  • A fingerprint of your PC and your IP address, kept only as scrambled (keyed hash) values, never as they came, so we can enforce the limit on accounts per PC and spot accounts that feed each other.

Discord, if you link it

  • Your Discord user ID and username, and whether you let other players see your username on your card.

On your own PC

  • The game keeps your local settings (like sound volume and interface size) and, if it crashes, a small crash log that it sends to us the next time it starts.

How we use it

  • To sign you in, save your character, and run online features: chat, friends, trading, clans, dungeons, referrals and announcements.
  • To show the hiscores, player cards and the Discord bot's posts about milestones and rare drops.
  • To keep the game fair and safe: the speed check, the limit on accounts per PC, reviewing reports, and moderation.
  • To answer support requests, restore lost progress and fix bugs.

We don't use your information for advertising, profiling or anything unrelated to running IdleBound.

What other players can see

  • Your character's name, levels, XP, total level, achievement points, rank, title, gear, appearance and portrait, clan and tag, game mode (Lone Wolf), when you started, and whether you're online.
  • Your Discord username, if you linked Discord and chose to show it.
  • What you say in public, clan and party chat.
  • Posts by the IdleBound Discord bot in our Discord server about milestones, rare drops, clan projects and dungeon clears, which name your character.

Services we use and when we share

  • Steam (Valve) confirms your sign-in and receives the “what I'm doing” status your Steam friends see, and your achievements once they go live on Steam.
  • Cloudflare hosts our game server and stores its data.
  • Discord: if Discord is running on your PC, the game shows your status there through Discord's own app. If you link your account, our Discord bot uses your Discord ID to give you roles in our server.
  • GitHub hosts this wiki.

We share information otherwise only when the law requires it, to protect players or the game from harm, or if IdleBound changes hands (and this policy would still apply). We never sell or rent personal information.

How long we keep it

  • Your save is kept while your account exists. We also keep backups: one an hour for the last 12 hours and one a day for the last 7 days, cut down to the latest 2 if you haven't played for 14 days.
  • Chat messages are deleted after 6 hours (and only the latest 50 are kept).
  • Your PC and address records are deleted 90 days after we last see them.
  • The Discord bot's feed of game events is deleted after 14 days. Posts already made in our Discord server stay there.
  • Sign-in tokens expire after 7 days.
  • Reports, moderation records, trade records and bug reports are kept as long as they're needed to keep the game safe and fair.

Your choices and rights

  • Reset your character any time from Settings → Account in the game.
  • Show or hide your Discord username in Settings → Community, or unlink Discord there.
  • Ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete your account entirely (your save, backups, hiscores and PC records). Contact us as described below. We may keep moderation records after a deletion so a banned player can't simply start again.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights over your information, including to object to how we use it or to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

IdleBound is not made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. Steam has its own age rules for accounts. If you believe a child under 13 is playing, contact us and we'll delete the account.

Where your information is processed

Our server runs on Cloudflare's network, so your information may be processed in the United States and other countries, which may have different data protection laws from yours.

Security

Connections to our server are encrypted, sign-in tokens are signed and expire, your Steam ID is never shown to other players, and PC and address records are stored only as keyed hashes. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect what we keep.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update it here and change the date at the top. Big changes are also announced in the game or on our Discord.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact staff on the IdleBound Discord.
